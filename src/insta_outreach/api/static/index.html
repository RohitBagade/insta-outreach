        1. The top box says in one sentence what the bot is doing right now.

        3. The coloured strip under the header appears only when something needs you: an approval, a reply or an Instagram problem.
        4. Today's progress is your to-do list for today; everything ticks itself off.
        5. Click any @name to see the chat and why the bot did each thing. Everything is also in the menu on the left.
